Privacy Policy for ReelSync

Effective Date: November 14, 2026  |  Developer: CBus22  |  Contact: [Your Support Email]

This Privacy Policy explains how CBus22 ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the ReelSync mobile application. By using ReelSync, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver cloud-synced watchlists, personalized discovery, and social features, ReelSync collects:

  • Account & Authentication Data: When you sign up or sign in, we collect your email address, display name/username, and authentication tokens managed via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Watchlists & User-Generated Content: We store your saved movies and TV shows, custom list names, episode watch progress, content-rating preferences (such as Family Friendly filters), ratings, and posts or comments you share in the Social feed.
  • App Diagnostics & Device Information: We collect basic technical logs, device model, Android OS version, and crash diagnostics to maintain stability and resolve bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Sync your watchlists, custom queues, and watch progress across your devices in real time.
  • Power personalized TV and movie recommendations through the Smart Tag Curator.
  • Display your profile, ratings, and spoiler-free posts to friends or the community when you choose to share them.
  • Secure user accounts, prevent abuse, and respond to support inquiries.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. ReelSync relies on the following trusted third-party services to operate:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore / Database, and Crashlytics): Used to authenticate users, securely store and sync watchlist and social data, and monitor app stability. Data is governed by the Firebase Privacy & Security Policy.
  • Media & Streaming Metadata Providers: Used to fetch public movie/TV synopses, ratings, posters, and streaming availability. Only title search queries and filter parameters are sent—never your personal account identity.

4. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the ReelSync app and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and encrypted at rest. We retain your account and watchlist data for as long as your account remains active or until you request deletion.

5. Children’s Privacy

Although ReelSync includes optional Family Friendly rating filters for household browsing, the app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has submitted personal data, contact us immediately so we can delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Account & Data Deletion Request

Users of ReelSync (developed by CBus22) can permanently delete their account and all associated cloud data at any time using either method below:

Option 1: Delete Directly in the App (Instant)

  • Open the ReelSync app and sign in to your account.
  • Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  • Select Settings > Delete Account and confirm your request.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you no longer have the app installed, click the button below to email our support team from the email address registered with your ReelSync account:

Request Account Deletion via Email

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted (within 30 days): Your Firebase Authentication record (email and user ID), profile details, saved watchlists, watch progress, and social posts/comments.
  • Retained: Anonymized, aggregated crash diagnostics that cannot be linked back to your identity.
